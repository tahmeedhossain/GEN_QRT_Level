Quantum Resource Network

Thirteen quantum resource theories — purity, athermality, asymmetry, coherence, imaginarity, magic, CV Wigner negativity, entanglement, steering, Bell nonlocality, contextuality, measurement incompatibility, and discord as the cautionary counterexample — compiled as one typed, signed graph. Each theory is a band of role-resolved nodes: free states vs resource states, free operations vs resource-utilizing operations vs impossible-even-with-resources, detector vs quantifier witnesses. Cross-theory edges land on the exact object they concern, and every conversion names the operation class (via) under which it holds. No edge means unsettled, never "false" — the open problems are enumerable mechanically. Companion to the Nonclassicality Network.

Built for three uses: automating research-idea generation (the 13×13 via-class conversion matrix is the queryable agenda: 13 of 156 directed cells charted), reviewing the field as curation (one claim = one edge with verbatim statement, references and a per-reference provenance tag), and visualizing where free sets overlap, where resources convert, where spending is powerful and where it is provably futile. The review process is itself the generator: surveying the field in this format yields its own research agenda as a by-product.

Derived propositions (QP1–QP5)

Assembled by closing gaps the compilation exposed; proofs/sketches in the catalog and the paper. No novelty pass has been run yet — QP2–QP4 are plausibly folklore; none is claimed as a first.

  • QP1 — Typology of free operations. The twelve proper theories split into symmetry-, subtheory-, partition- and constraint-type by how their free operations are physically justified; athermality is the unique two-type intersection (Gibbs constraint ∩ U(1)-covariance), and the intersection reappears verbatim at state level (unique free state γ) and witness level (free-energy family + asymmetry-mode family, jointly complete, separately incomplete).
  • QP2 — Tensor-stability failure of state-level Bell locality. Superactivation makes tensor powers of Bell-local entangled states nonlocal, so the monoidal axiom fails for "local states"; it holds for common-cause boxes under LOSR — when an axiom fails, the objects were wrong.
  • QP3 — Bound resources are operation-class-relative. Entanglement has bound states (PPT), magic has them (Wigner-positive), thermal-covariant coherence is all bound (full rank), IO-coherence has none, purity has none: "boundness" is a joint property of (F, O), not of the free set alone.
  • QP4 — The purity → coherence → entanglement cascade. P ≥ C_max (via free unitaries, exact for distance-based quantifiers) and C ≥ E_max (via bipartite incoherent operations): one quantitative chain of generation bounds across three theories, with each link's operation class stated.
  • QP5 — Thermal magic. For a generic interacting Hamiltonian the Gibbs state is outside the stabilizer polytope: the unique free state of athermality — also free for coherence, asymmetry and imaginarity in the energy basis — is a magic resource. Quantifying mana(γ(β)) is gap QG5.

Registered gaps (QG1–QG10)

  1. Multiqubit (even-d) magic ⇔ contextuality: exact scope without a covariant Wigner function.
  2. Is TO = GPO ∩ TIO? The strict inclusions are settled; the exact characterization is not.
  3. NCW|Bell ≠ LOSR: what single free-operation class unifies contextuality and nonlocality, and which monotones survive both?
  4. The reversibility-frontier table: for each theory, (exactly-RNG reversible? asymptotically-RNG reversible? physical-class reversible?). Known points: ENT (no/yes/no), IO-coherence (no for cost/yes/partial), thermal asymmetry (no, maximally), magic (no under STAB/axioms to check), purity (yes/yes/yes).
  5. Uncharted conversion cells: thermal magic, real thermal operations, imaginarity cost of T states — the 13×13 via-class matrix is the automation hook.
  6. A steering analogue of the entangleability of cones.
  7. An erasure-cost calculus beyond entanglement (asymmetry, magic, imaginarity).
  8. A maximal physically motivated operation class for non-convex free sets (the discord repair).
  9. Catalytic/battery routes around exact-RNG irreversibility.
  10. Thermodynamics of bound entanglement — and now of bound magic.
