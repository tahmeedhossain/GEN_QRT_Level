# Changelog — Quantum Resource Network (GEN_QRT_Level)

Every released version stays online unchanged; a correction is a new version alongside.

## v1.2 — 2026-11-01 (current; `qrt_v12.html`, `data/qrt_network_v1.2.json`)

From round 4 of the combined paper *The Great Epistemic Network* (literature searched before each proof) and from writing
out the proofs of QP1–QP5 in full:

- New conversion edge **C20** `sR-ath → sR-mag` (converts_to, possibility; via thermal operations, qubit analysis; literature fill:
  de Oliveira Junior, Macedo, Czartowski, Brask, Chaves, arXiv:2511.13839 (2025)). Charts the athermality → magic cell:
  14 of 156 directed conversion cells charted (v1.1: 13).
- **QP1** (correction): the constraint axiom alone already has the free set {γ}; the state-level shadow of the double
  justification of athermality is the inclusion of {γ} in the symmetric states, not a reduction of two large sets to one point.
- **QG2** (update): answered negatively for a qutrit with equally spaced levels — Ding, Ding, Hu, PRA 103, 052214 (2021);
  what remains open is a characterization of the gap between thermal and enhanced thermal operations.
- **QP5**, **QG5** (notes): magic generation by thermal operations is treated for a qubit in arXiv:2511.13839 (C20);
  the Gibbs-state statement of QP5 lies within that question but was not found there in explicit form; the general
  conditional statement of QP5 stands.
- Census: 116 nodes, 86 edges (39 possibility, 13 no-go, 34 structural).
