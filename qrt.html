Quantum Resource Network

Thirteen quantum resource theories as one typed graph — fourth instance of the observable–model–state network. Each theory is one horizontal band; the columns inside it split the layers the way the theories actually work: model (defining axioms) · states, split into the free set, the resource states and a distinguished shared state · processes, split into free operations (cannot create the resource), resource-utilizing operations (what spending it buys) and impossible processes (no-go even with the resource) · witnesses, split into detectors and quantifiers. Cross-theory edges now land on the exact node they concern: a free-set overlap meets a free-set node, a conversion runs resource-to-resource and names its via operation class.
