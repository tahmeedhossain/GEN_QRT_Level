# Quantum Resource Theory Network — Catalog (QRT-Net v1)

Date: 2026-10-23. Fourth instance of the observable–model–state epistemic network (after the classical EM network and the nonclassicality networks v1/v2). Companion data file: `qrt_network.json`. Interactive page: published as a separate artifact.

**Scope.** Thirteen resource theories as columns, four node rows: **model** (defining axioms: free set F, free operations O, golden unit, monotones), **states** (free/resource split, distinguished shared states), **processes** (CPTP free-operation classes and their lattice), **witnesses & tasks** (observables detecting the resource; operational tasks it powers). Edges state: (i) axiom overlap/independence at the model level and its translation downward; (ii) set relations among free sets; (iii) conversions "resource of A → resource of B", always annotated with the operation class `via` under which the conversion holds; (iv) witness/task attachments.

**Conversion convention.** "The resource of theory A generates the resource of theory B" is not well-formed until an operation class is fixed (free ops of B, an intersection class, or a physically motivated class). Every conversion edge in the JSON carries a `via` field. This is the single most common source of confusion in the cross-theory literature and the network makes it explicit.

## 2. Model layer: axiom overlaps and independence, and how they translate downward

This is the section the user asked for explicitly: which defining axioms of the theories coincide, intersect, or are independent, and what that implies at the state and process levels.

### 2.1 Four justification types (QP1, derived)

Free operations are justified in four distinct ways:

1. **Symmetry type** — O = channels covariant under a group representation. Members: asymmetry (any G); unspeakable coherence (G = U(1) translations generated by H); imaginarity is the ℤ₂ *antiunitary* analogue (not literally covariance, since conjugation K is not a channel; free ops are real-Kraus channels — Hickey & Gour, J. Phys. A 51, 414009 (2018)).
2. **Subtheory type** — O = operations of a classically simulable subtheory. Members: magic (stabilizer subtheory, Gottesman–Knill); CV non-Gaussianity (Gaussian subtheory; positive-Wigner simulability, Mari–Eisert, PRL 109, 230503 (2012)).
3. **Partition type** — O = operations compatible with a causal/communication partition. Members: entanglement (LOCC), steering (1W-LOCC), nonlocality (LOSR wirings), contextuality (noncontextual wirings), incompatibility (classical programmability).
4. **Constraint type** — O = channels preserving a distinguished state. Members: purity (I/d), athermality (γ).

**The one genuine intersection: athermality = constraint ∩ symmetry.** Thermal operations are simultaneously Gibbs-preserving (constraint: TO(γ)=γ) and time-translation covariant (symmetry: energy-conserving unitaries commute with e^{−iHt}).

**Translation to the state level.** The free set of athermality is the unique state that is both a fixed point of the constraint *and* symmetric: γ is U(1)-invariant and Gibbs. Nothing else survives both axioms — which is why athermality has a one-point free set while each parent axiom alone leaves a large one (all Gibbs-preserving fixed points on one side, all block-diagonal states on the other).

**Translation to the process/witness level.** The complete set of second-law constraints *factors* along the two axioms: the Rényi free energies F_α govern the block-diagonal (constraint) data (Brandão, Horodecki, Ng, Oppenheim, Wehner, PNAS 112, 3275 (2015)), while the coherences are governed by independent asymmetry-mode constraints (Lostaglio, Korzekwa, Jennings, Rudolph, PRX 5, 021001 (2015), verified). Neither family alone is complete; work can even be *locked* in coherence (Lostaglio, Jennings, Rudolph, Nat. Commun. 6, 6383 (2015)). And the two parent operation classes separate strictly in the quantum regime: a Gibbs-preserving map can create energy coherence while a thermal operation cannot (Faist, Oppenheim, Renner, NJP 17, 043003 (2015), verified). Whether TO = GPO ∩ TIO exactly is left open here (gap QG2).

### 2.2 Asymmetry vs coherence: the speakable/unspeakable split

- **Axiom level.** Unspeakable coherence = asymmetry under translations (Marvian–Spekkens, PRA 94, 052324 (2016), verified). Speakable coherence (IO/MIO framework of Baumgratz–Cramer–Plenio) has *no* symmetry justification; Marvian–Spekkens proposed DIO as an alternative; TIO ⊂ DIO strictly (group averaging implements dephasing covariance).
- **State level.** Symmetric states = incoherent in the (nondegenerate) energy basis — the free sets coincide; the *monotone* sets do not: measures of speakable coherence are a proper subset of measures of asymmetry (Marvian–Spekkens–Zanardi, PRA 93, 052331 (2016), verified).
- **Process level, dramatic divergence (QP3 input).** Under IO, *every* coherent state is distillable at rate C_r (Winter–Yang, PRL 116, 120404 (2016)) — no bound coherence. Under thermal/covariant processing, distillable coherence is **zero** for all full-rank states while the coherence *cost* equals the QFI (Marvian, Nat. Commun. 11, 25 (2020), verified) — everything is bound. Same free set, different O, opposite phenomenology. This is the sharpest single illustration in the network of an axiom-level distinction (IO vs TIO) becoming a state-level dichotomy (reversible-ish vs maximally irreversible).

### 2.3 The partition ladder

LO ⊂ LOSR ⊂ 1W-LOCC ⊂ LOCC ⊂ SEP-ops ⊂ PPT-ops ⊂ NE. Tightening the communication structure of the *same* bipartite scenario produces nonlocality, steering, entanglement as three distinct theories; the free sets nest accordingly at state level (SEP ⊊ unsteerable ⊊ Bell-local; strictness by Werner states — Werner, PRA 40, 4277 (1989); Barrett, PRA 65, 042302 (2002); Wiseman–Jones–Doherty, PRL 98, 140402 (2007); general measurements Quintino et al., PRA 92, 032107 (2015)). LOCC is the *wrong* class for Bell scenarios (communication can fake correlations): the resource of nonlocality is LOSR-entanglement (Wolfe et al., Quantum 4, 280 (2020), verified; Schmid–Selby–Wolfe–Spekkens, arXiv:1911.12462 [verify published venue]).

Contextuality's noncontextual wirings unify KS and Bell scenarios (Amaral, Cabello, Terra Cunha, Aolita, PRL 120, 130403 (2018), verified), **but** NCW restricted to Bell scenarios ≠ LOSR (Santos, Wagner, Amaral, arXiv:2407.02120, verified) — the two "classical operations" proposals disagree (gap QG3).

Incompatibility sits in this family as a *device* resource: free devices are classically programmable; convertibility is completely characterized by guessing games with post-measurement information (Buscemi, Chitambar, Zhou, PRL 124, 120401 (2020), verified). Its state-layer shadow is the exact steering correspondence: every steering problem ↔ joint-measurability problem, one-to-one (Uola, Budroni, Gühne, Pellonpää, PRL 115, 230402 (2015), verified).

### 2.4 Subtheory twins

Magic (DV) and Wigner negativity (CV) share one mechanism: a quasiprobability representation whose positivity cone contains the free set and bounds simulability. Odd d: mana = 0 ⇔ discrete-Wigner-positive; Wigner-positive non-stabilizer states are **bound magic** (undistillable; Veitch, Ferrie, Gross, Emerson, NJP 14, 113011 (2012)). CV: positive Wigner ⇒ efficient classical simulation (Mari–Eisert); Wigner negativity ⇔ CV contextuality (Booth, Chabaud, Émeriau, PRL 129, 230401 (2022)); and in DV, mana > 0 ⇒ contextuality w.r.t. stabilizer measurements, necessary (and sufficient in odd prime d) for distillability (Howard, Wallman, Veitch, Emerson, Nature 510, 351 (2014)). The multiqubit case remains obstructed by state-independent contextuality — inherited gap (QG1 here, G3 in the nonclassicality network).

### 2.5 Framework axioms and their violators

- **Convexity** (F convex, closed) ⇒ witnesses exist (Hahn–Banach) ⇒ generalized robustness well-defined ⇒ **robustness = exact advantage in subchannel discrimination** (Takagi, Regula, Bu, Liu, Adesso, PRL 122, 140402 (2019), verified; Takagi–Regula, PRX 9, 031053 (2019), verified). One axiom, three layers.
- **Tensor axiom** (F⊗F ⊆ F; CFS monoidal structure — Coecke–Fritz–Spekkens, Inf. Comput. 250, 59 (2016)): holds for SEP, incoherent, symmetric, real, STAB, Wigner-positive. **Fails** for state-level Bell locality: superactivation (Palazuelos, PRL 109, 190401 (2012)) makes tensor powers of a local entangled state nonlocal (QP2). Resolution: the objects of the nonlocality theory are boxes, where LOSR closure holds.
- **Stein-lemma axioms** (Brandão–Plenio): generalized quantum Stein's lemma — claimed 2010, gap found 2023 (Berta et al., Quantum 7, 1103 (2023) [verify page]), **proven 2024** (Hayashi–Yamasaki, arXiv:2408.02722; Lami, arXiv:2408.06410; both verified) ⇒ second law of QRTs under asymptotically resource-non-generating operations with the regularized relative entropy of resource as the unique rate function. This **coexists** with irreversibility under *exactly* non-entangling operations (Lami–Regula, Nat. Phys. 19, 184 (2023), verified): the exact/asymptotic non-generation distinction is the load-bearing hinge (gap QG4: map the frontier per theory).
- **Discord violates two axioms**: CC states are non-convex (mixing classical states of different bases is discordant); local channels create discord (Ciccarello–Giovannetti, PRA 85, 010102(R) (2012) [verify]; Hu et al., PRA 85, 032102 (2012) [verify]) — no golden-rule class containing local processing. Yet the advantage reading survives without convexity (Kuroiwa, Takagi, Adesso, Yamasaki, "Every quantum helps" [verify venue]): discord-type quantumness still buys multicopy discrimination advantage, and it *activates* into distillable entanglement (below).

Reading: the free sets of the thirteen theories are pairwise transversal — **no containment holds between SEP and STAB** (Bell state vs product non-stabilizer state), the free set of one theory routinely contains maximal resources of another (|Φ⁺⟩ is magic-free and imaginarity-free), and the unique free state of athermality is generically a magic resource (QP5). The only genuine containments: {I/d} ⊂ everything; incoherent(product basis) ⊂ SEP; symmetric = incoherent(energy basis, nondeg. H); SEP ⊂ unsteerable ⊂ Bell-local.

**QP5 proof sketch (thermal magic).** STAB is a polytope of measure zero boundary structure in state space; γ(β) = e^{−βH}/Z traces an analytic curve from I/d (β=0, in STAB) toward the ground state. For generic interacting H the ground state is not a stabilizer state, so the curve exits STAB at some finite β*; for β > β*, mana/robustness of magic of γ is positive. Literature on quantifying this ("thermal magic") not located in this pass → gap QG5. (Not claimed novel; claimed *uncompiled*.)

## 4. Conversion edges (each with its `via` class)

1. **Coherence → entanglement**, via bipartite incoherent operations: faithful (possible iff input coherent); E_r(out) ≤ C_r(in), achievable by generalized CNOT. Streltsov, Singh, Dhar, Bera, Adesso, PRL 115, 020403 (2015) — verified.
2. **Entanglement → coherence**, via LQICC/LICC intersection classes: assisted distillation/localization of coherence consuming shared entanglement. Chitambar–Hsieh, PRL 117, 020402 (2016); Chitambar et al., PRL 116, 070402 (2016) [verify author list]; Streltsov et al., PRL 116, 240405 (2016) [verify].
3. **Purity → coherence**, via unitaries (free in purity): purity = maximal unitarily achievable coherence, exactly (distance-based quantifiers coincide); purity also bounds unitary entanglement/discord generation — the cascade QP4: P ≥ C_max ≥ E_max with each link's class stated. Streltsov, Kampermann, Wölk, Gessner, Bruß, NJP 20, 053058 (2018) — verified.
4. **Asymmetry ↔ unspeakable coherence**: identification, not conversion (Sec. 2.2).
5. **Coherence as athermality resource**, via TO: mode constraints, work locking, transfer irreversibility beyond free energy. PRX 5, 021001 (2015) — verified.
6. **Asymmetry under thermal processing is maximally irreversible**: cost = QFI, distillable = 0 (full rank). Marvian, Nat. Commun. 11, 25 (2020) — verified.
7. **Discord → entanglement (activation)**, via local CNOTs with ancillas + distillation: every discordant state (incl. separable) generates distillable entanglement; REQ = minimal distillable entanglement generated; discord = minimal entanglement created in a measurement. Piani et al., PRL 106, 220403 (2011) — verified; Streltsov–Kampermann–Bruß, PRL 106, 160401 (2011).
8. **Entanglement → nonlocality**, via local measurements: necessary, insufficient per copy (Werner/Barrett); superactivatable (Palazuelos); every entangled state wins a semiquantum game (Buscemi, PRL 108, 200401 (2012)).
9. **Entanglement → steering**, via incompatible measurements on the untrusted wing; strict hierarchy both directions (Sec. 2.3). Steering requires entanglement **and** incompatibility jointly.
10. **Magic → contextuality**, via stabilizer measurements (odd prime d: equivalence to distillability; Howard et al. 2014). CV twin: negativity ⇔ contextuality (Booth et al. 2022).
11. **Coherence necessary, not sufficient, for magic** (derived + arXiv:1801.04807 [verify authors/venue]): a state diagonal in a stabilizer basis is a mixture of stabilizer states, hence in STAB; so nonstabilizerness ⇒ coherence in every stabilizer basis; |+⟩ kills the converse; no faithful conversion protocol known → QG5.
12. **Imaginarity → local discrimination power**, via LOCC vs real-LOCC: real orthogonal pure-state pairs perfectly LOCC-distinguishable but indistinguishable under local real operations. Wu et al., PRL 126, 090401 (2021); PRA 103, 032401 (2021) — both verified.
13. **Athermality → work** (second laws, PNAS 2015); **correlations → work** (Perarnau-Llobet et al., PRX 5, 041011 (2015) [verify]).
14. **Catalysis** as a cross-theory operations phenomenon: entanglement (Jonathan–Plenio, PRL 83, 3566 (1999)), coherence (Åberg, PRL 113, 150402 (2014)), thermodynamics; review Datta, Kondra, Miller, Streltsov, Rep. Prog. Phys. 86, 116002 (2023) — verified. Candidate route around exact-NE irreversibility: entanglement-battery second law (arXiv:2405.10599 [verify]) → QG9.

## 5. Derived propositions (assembled here; proof sketches above; not claimed to be in the literature as stated)

- **QP1** — Typology of free-op justifications; athermality as the unique intersection type; factorization reappears at state and witness levels (Sec. 2.1).
- **QP2** — Tensor-axiom failure for state-level Bell locality; repaired by changing objects to boxes (Sec. 2.5).
- **QP3** — Bound resources are operation-class-relative: ENT has them (PPT), MAG has them (W₊), thermal-covariant coherence is *all* bound, IO-coherence has *none*, purity has none. "Boundness" is a property of (F, O), not of F (Sec. 2.2, 2.4).
- **QP4** — Quantitative cascade P ≥ C_max ≥ E_max with explicit via-classes (Sec. 4.3).
- **QP5** — Thermal magic: γ of generic interacting H is magic-resourced while free in four other theories (Sec. 3).

## 8. v1.1 structure note (2026-10-23)

The node set was refined after review. Each theory band now carries: **free-state** and **resource-state** nodes (so free-set overlaps land on free sets and conversions run resource→resource); **three process nodes** — free operations (cannot create the resource), resource-utilizing operations (what spending the resource enables: injection, activation, work extraction, discrimination advantages), and impossible processes (no-go theorems that hold even with the resource: pX-ath thermal coherence distillation/work locking, pX-coh IO mixed-state irreversibility, pX-ent no second law under exact NE + bound entanglement, pX-mag bound magic + no unit-rate interconversion); and **two witness nodes** — detector vs quantifier (made load-bearing by Wolfe et al.: facet Bell violations detect but do not quantify). New free-set overlap edges: SS3b, SS4b, SS5b, SS12–SS19 (e.g. {γ} ⊂ incoherent(energy), INC ⊂ real, SEP ⊂ unsteerable ⊂ Bell-local, Bell-local ⊂ noncontextual per scenario, STAB/SEP transversality); new conversion C19 (incompatibility → steering, constructive direction of the Uola correspondence). Totals: 116 nodes, 85 edges. All v1 statements, references and provenance tags carried over unchanged.
